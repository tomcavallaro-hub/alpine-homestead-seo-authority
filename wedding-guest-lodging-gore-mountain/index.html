Where to put the wedding party for a Gore Mountain wedding

Gore Mountain's own wedding materials describe mountaintop ceremonies reached by the gondola and receptions in the base-area Tannery hall. What they don't provide is beds. Gore refers couples to local lodging instead. If you're the couple, a parent or the maid of honor trying to keep the wedding party under one roof, The Alpine Homestead™ in Olmstedville sleeps 12.

Who this house suits

  • The wedding party. Bridesmaids, groomsmen or the couple's immediate families in one place, so nobody misses the hair-and-makeup start time.
  • One family branch. Grandparents, siblings and kids traveling together for a wedding at Gore or elsewhere in the area.
  • A micro-wedding. The host describes the 1.6-acre lawn, wraparound porch and common rooms as a setting for small ceremonies and celebrations. Ask the host directly about your plans before booking.

Getting everyone there and back

The host lists the drive to Gore Mountain at roughly 8 to 10 minutes. Gore's base area is in North Creek. Line up sober drivers for the reception before the weekend starts. Mountain roads at night are no place to sort that out. Off-street parking has room for several vehicles.

Questions to ask before you reserve

  • Are your wedding dates inside a weekend or holiday minimum-stay window?
  • Will guests share beds? The six beds are a King, two Queens, two Fulls and a Twin.
  • Are you planning any event at the house itself? Tell the host your plans and headcount up front.
  • Do you need a high chair or pack-and-play for young guests? The host lists both.

The host's own wedding and celebration weekends page covers what the house offers for events.

Quick answers

Does Gore Mountain offer lodging for wedding guests?
Gore Mountain's wedding guide says it provides referrals to local lodging and rentals; it does not list lodging of its own.
How many wedding guests can stay at The Alpine Homestead?
Up to 12 guests, in 5 bedrooms with 4 bathrooms.
Can we hold a small wedding at the house?
The host says the property suits micro-weddings and small celebrations. Contact the host with your dates and group size for details.
What are check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is 4:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Plan the wedding-day schedule around those times.
