Lodging for a Hudson River Gorge rafting trip near North Creek

The Hudson River Gorge is one of the East's best-known whitewater runs, and trips are usually booked by groups. That leaves you with twelve wet, hungry people to house. The Alpine Homestead™ in Olmstedville is a 5-bedroom basecamp a short drive from the North Creek outfitters.

How release days work

Outside spring melt, the Gorge depends on water released from the Lake Abanakee dam on the Indian River near Indian Lake. American Whitewater lists those releases on Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday mornings, May through September. Book your trip on a release day, and confirm the schedule with your outfitter, since it can change.

The trip itself

North Creek Rafting Company, the host's rafting partner, lists its Hudson River Gorge day trip at 17 miles of Class III to IV water. It also offers overnight trips and a 5-mile Class I lazy-river tubing run for anyone who'd rather float. No prior rafting experience is needed for guided trips; outfitters supply a New York State licensed guide, equipment and instruction.

The state tourism site lists Gorge outfitters in several towns:

  • North Creek: North Creek Rafting Company, Whitewater Challengers, Adirondack Adventures
  • North River: Adirondack River Outfitters
  • Indian Lake: Adirondac Rafting Company, Adventure Sports Rafting Company
  • Warrensburg: Wild Waters Outdoor Center

Ask each outfitter where trips meet, since meeting points and drive times differ.

Why a house beats a motel after a river day

  • Four bathrooms for twelve people coming off the river at the same time.
  • A full kitchen for one big dinner, or a private chef arranged through the host.
  • A fire pit for retelling the rapids. The host supplies wood for the first fire.
  • Parking with room for several cars, so the group can split up for different outfitters.

Packing list for rafters

  • A full change of dry clothes and a towel to leave in the car
  • Footwear that stays on in water; ask your outfitter what they allow
  • Groceries bought on the way in; TOPS in North Creek is the nearest full grocery
  • For spring trips, ask about wetsuit or drysuit rentals before you arrive

Quick answers

When is the whitewater biggest on the Hudson Gorge?
In spring, when snowmelt raises the river; the state tourism site says rafting season typically begins around the second week of April. Summer trips rely on scheduled dam releases and run gentler.
Which days do summer releases happen?
American Whitewater lists releases from the Lake Abanakee dam on the Indian River on Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday mornings in season. Confirm your date with your outfitter.
Is there a minimum age for rafting?
Age and weight minimums are set by each outfitter and depend on the season and trip. Ask the outfitter before booking.
How many rafters can stay at The Alpine Homestead?
Up to 12 guests, in 5 bedrooms with 4 bathrooms.
