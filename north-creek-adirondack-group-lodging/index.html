What North Creek gives a group

  • Gore Mountain and the Ski Bowl. Gore's original base camp, the North Creek Ski Bowl, dates to 1934 and sits near the village.
  • Rail history. North Creek was the northern end of the first railroad into the Adirondacks. In 1901, Vice President Theodore Roosevelt raced from Mount Marcy to the North Creek station as President McKinley lay dying.
  • Rail biking. Revolution Rail runs pedal rail-bike tours from North Creek Station; its site lists a 7-mile round-trip North Run and an 8-mile pedal-assist South Run along the Hudson. See the host's rail biking page.
  • Culture on a rainy day. The North Creek Depot Museum and the Tannery Pond Community Center.
  • Supplies. TOPS Markets for groceries, and restaurants including The Barking Spider on Main Street, from the host's local guide.

Quick answers

Is The Alpine Homestead in North Creek?
No. It is in Olmstedville, a hamlet in the town of Minerva, Essex County. North Creek is in the neighboring town of Johnsburg, Warren County. Both are inside the Adirondack Park.
How far is North Creek from the house?
The host puts the TOPS grocery in North Creek about 10 to 15 minutes away.
What kinds of groups stay here?
Family reunions, multi-family trips, friend groups and small celebrations. It sleeps up to 12 in 5 bedrooms with 4 bathrooms.
Is there Wi-Fi for remote work?
The host lists high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the house.
