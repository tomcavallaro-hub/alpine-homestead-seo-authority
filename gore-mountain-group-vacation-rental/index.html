Booking one house for a group of 12 near Gore Mountain

The person who organizes a group trip to Gore Mountain usually ends up answering the same questions from everyone: Where do I sleep? How many bathrooms? Who's driving? This guide answers them for The Alpine Homestead™, a circa-1850 farmhouse in Olmstedville, New York.

  • 5Bedrooms
  • 4Bathrooms
  • 12Guests max
  • 1.6Acres

Photos, the full amenity list and direct booking are on the official site: thealpinehomestead.com.

Beds first, headcount second

Group trips fall apart over beds more often than over cost. The host publishes this layout:

Six beds hold twelve people only when most of them share. Five couples plus two singles fits. Twelve friends who each want a bed does not. Settle room assignments before anyone pays a deposit.

The bathroom math

Four full bathrooms for twelve guests is three people per bathroom. That ratio is what keeps a 7 a.m. ski morning from turning into a line in the hallway,.

Splitting the house fairly

Rates for your dates appear only on the official booking page, so this guide lists no prices. Two common ways groups divide the total:

  • By room: the King and Queen rooms pay a little more than the Fulls.
  • By person: simplest when the group is mostly couples.

Whichever you choose, collect each share before booking. The minimum stay is 2 nights, or 3 on weekends and holidays.

Cars, parking and the drive

The host lists the drive to Gore Mountain at roughly 8 to 10 minutes. Plan at least two or three cars for twelve people. The property has off-street parking with space for several vehicles, plus room for trailers, snowmobiles and motorcycles.

Meals for twelve

The kitchen is fully equipped, and the host's list includes a dishwasher, ice maker, stand mixer, slow cooker and coffee grinder. The nearest full grocery is TOPS Markets in North Creek, which the host puts 10 to 15 minutes away. Assign one dinner per couple and the cooking stays manageable. For one night off, the host can arrange a private chef, depending on the chef's schedule.

When people want different things

Mixed groups split up during the day. Skiers head to Gore Mountain. Others pick from the host's Stay & Play™ list of local outfitters, including rail biking out of North Creek and guided outings. In the evening everyone is back at the wood stove, the foosball table or the fire pit.

Quick answers

How many people can the house sleep?
Up to 12 guests, in 5 bedrooms with 4 bathrooms.
Will 12 single adults each get their own bed?
No. There are six beds: a King, two Queens, two Fulls and a Twin. Twelve guests means most beds are shared, so the house suits couples and families best.
How far is Gore Mountain?
The host lists the drive at roughly 8 to 10 minutes. It is not ski-in or walking distance.
Is there room for several cars?
Yes. The host lists off-street parking for multiple vehicles, plus trailers, snowmobiles and motorcycles.
