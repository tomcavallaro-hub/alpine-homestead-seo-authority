A ski vacation near Gore Mountain for the whole extended family

A ski trip with toddlers, teenagers and a grandparent who'd rather read by the fire needs a different plan than a weekend with friends. Here's how a large family can run a Gore Mountain ski trip from The Alpine Homestead™, a 5-bedroom house in Olmstedville.

  • 5Bedrooms
  • 4Bathrooms
  • 12Guests max
  • 1.6Acres

Check family-friendly details and book direct at thealpinehomestead.com.

Lessons for every age

Gore Mountain's lessons are grouped by age:

  • Children's group lessons, ages 4 to 12
  • Daily adult and teen lessons, ages 13 and up
  • Private lessons for any age, and adaptive lessons for skiers with disabilities, booked by phone

Book lessons ahead of holiday weeks through Gore's lessons page, which also lists current prices.

A day that works for everyone

  1. Morning: four bathrooms for the rush, then a short drive. The host lists it at roughly 8 to 10 minutes to Gore.
  2. Midday: younger kids in lessons while older skiers explore. Non-skiers can try the cross-country and snowshoe trails at Gore's Nordic Center at the North Creek Ski Bowl.
  3. Late afternoon: naps, foosball and board games at the house.
  4. Evening: Gore says lift tickets include night access at the North Creek Ski Bowl, so teenagers can get a few more runs.

Gear and drying out

Renting saves hauling skis for a whole family. The host's ski rental page points to Cunningham's Ski Barn for rentals on the way to the mountain. Back at the house, the wood-burning stove is the gathering spot after a cold day.

Feeding the crew on a ski week

Stock up at TOPS in North Creek, which the host puts 10 to 15 minutes away, or at the larger Price Chopper in Warrensburg, about 25 minutes. A slow cooker is on the kitchen list: start dinner before the first chairlift and it's ready at the end of the day.

Before you book a holiday week: weekends and holidays carry a 3-night minimum, check-out is 10:00 AM, and pets are allowed with a fee. Read the cancellation policy on the booking page. Popular ski weeks fill early.

Quick answers

Does Gore Mountain teach kids?
Yes. Gore lists group lessons for children ages 4 to 12, daily lessons for ages 13 and up, private lessons for all ages, and adaptive lessons.
Is there baby gear at the house?
The host lists a high chair and a pack-and-play.
Can we ski at night?
Gore says all lift tickets and season passes include night access at the North Creek Ski Bowl. Check the current schedule before you go.
How many people does the house sleep?
Up to 12 guests, in 5 bedrooms with 4 bathrooms.
